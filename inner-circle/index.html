Quick answers

 

Do my points expire?

Nope. Your Glow Points stay put until you spend them — no countdown, no pressure.

How do I claim a reward?

Drop a message in the Glow Up Society or reply to any Glam email. We'll send your discount code or free product right over.

I bought from Glam before this launched. Do I get points?

Yes — every past purchase counts toward your starting balance.

Can I earn points without buying anything?

Absolutely. Showing up to lives, bringing friends, and posting your wins all earn points — that's the whole point of the Inner Circle.

How does VIP work? Do I spend my 300 points?

Nope — VIP is a status, not a purchase. The moment your lifetime points reach 300, you're VIP automatically and your points stay right where they are. VIPs get every new drop free, 25% off everything always, a monthly spotlight in the group, a vote on what Glam makes next, and a VIP badge in the Skool community.

How do I check my points balance?

Open your rewards app and type in the email you shop with — your Glow Points, rewards, and recent activity show up instantly. You can also ask in the Glow Up Society anytime.
