How this works

  1. Tell it about your product (Step 1) — name, who it's for, what makes it great.
  2. Copy the Campaign Strategist prompt (Step 2) into ChatGPT or Gemini. It does the deep research, strategy, and ad copy for you.
  3. Build your ad copy kit (Step 3) from what the AI gives you.
  4. Generate your 8-second video ad prompt (Step 4) and run it in Google Flow, Vidu, Runway, Pika, or Kling.
  5. Download your landing page pack (Step 5) — every word your page needs, plus a ready-to-edit HTML starter.
