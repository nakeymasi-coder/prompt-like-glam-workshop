What is this?

Video Marketer Studio is a guided studio that walks you through building a full video marketing campaign for your product — the same process the pros use, broken into 5 simple steps. You don't need any marketing experience. If you can answer questions about your own product, you can do this.

Good to know: nothing you type ever leaves your browser. The studio is a single page that runs entirely on your device — your ideas stay yours.

1 Tell it about your product

Enter your product name, a one-sentence description of what it does, who it's for, and pick up to 3 core benefits plus your campaign tone (bold & glam, warm & friendly, luxury…). Everything you enter here flows into every step after it, so take a minute to get this right.

2 Copy your Campaign Strategist prompt

Hit the button and the studio writes you a complete strategist prompt with your product details baked in. Copy it, paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini (free works fine), and let the AI do the heavy lifting: it researches your market and competitors, builds your strategy, writes your ad copy, plans your video shots, and hands you 3 next steps.

Tip: read the AI's answer and pick the one angle that excites you most — that's your campaign's winning idea for Step 3.

3 Build your ad copy kit

Type in your winning angle, pick a hook style (curiosity, bold claim, relatable pain…), and add your offer. The studio assembles your complete kit: a scroll-stopping hook, 3 headline options, your primary ad text, and your call to action — each with its own copy button.

4 Generate your 8-second video ad prompt

An 8-second ad runs in three beats: the hook (0–2 seconds), the proof (2–5 seconds), and the call to action (5–8 seconds). Describe what we see in each shot, add your on-screen text, pick a mood — and you get one copy-ready prompt to paste into Google Flow, Vidu, Runway, Pika, or Kling.

Tip: generate 2–3 versions and keep the one with the strongest first two seconds. That's what stops the scroll.

5 Get your landing page pack

Every word your landing page needs: sticky header text, hero headline and subheadline, a 3-point value prop grid, your video section caption, call to action, and footer line. Paste the blocks into any page builder — or hit Download HTML starter for a clean one-page template with everything filled in, ready to customize.

Quick answers

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. The studio is free. ChatGPT and Gemini both have free plans that handle the strategist prompt, and most video tools have a free tier to start.

I'm not techy. Can I still do this?

Yes — that's who it was built for. Just follow the steps top to bottom. If you get stuck, ask inside The Glam Vault community.

Where does my information go?

Nowhere. Everything runs in your own browser. The only time your words leave the page is when you choose to paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini, or a video tool.

Can I use this for client work?

Absolutely. Run a campaign per product — the studio starts fresh every time you open it.
