Make it yours — add your own queen

Create a new queen with this simple 3-part formula. She joins the list in Step 1, works exactly like the others — and she's saved automatically.

1. NAME HER
Give the category a proud name.
Examples: "Osteoporosis Queen", "Rosacea Queen", "Tourette Queen", "Scleroderma Warrior", "Myasthenia Queen".
2. DESCRIBE HER WITH DIGNITY
Beauty FIRST, strength ALWAYS, pity NEVER. No sad or weepy words. Template: "a [uplifting adjective] [person], [what makes her radiant], [her strength]".
3. USE HER
Tap the button below — she joins your queen list, saves automatically, and you can build prompts with her right away.

💛 Beauty FIRST · strength ALWAYS · pity NEVER — no sad, weepy, or sorrowful words, ever.

GOOD ✓
"a radiant queen living with chronic illness, her quiet strength glowing through every portrait, unbothered and luminous"
