The Method: The 5-Piece Formula

🧩 How every prompt is built

Every portrait prompt is built from 5 pieces, in this order:

[QUEEN] + [BEAUTY DETAILS] + [POWER WORD] + [TYPOGRAPHY] + [VIBE]

Each bracket is a menu of choices. Swap the choices, get a brand-new portrait. That is what makes it a generator instead of a single prompt.

👑 The Golden Rule (never break it): every description leads with BEAUTY, carries STRENGTH, and never touches PITY. No sad words. No weepy words. We celebrate — we don't mourn.

Create Your Own Queen — The Worksheet

👑 Add any queen you want to celebrate

  1. Name her — give the group a proud, celebratory name.
  2. Describe her with dignity — beauty FIRST, strength ALWAYS, pity NEVER. ("A radiant queen living with chronic illness, her quiet strength glowing through every portrait" — never "a poor sick woman suffering.")
  3. Test her — plug her into the template. Does she sound like a QUEEN? If anything sounds sad, rewrite until it shines.
  4. Add her to your list — give her the next number and use her exactly like the starters.
