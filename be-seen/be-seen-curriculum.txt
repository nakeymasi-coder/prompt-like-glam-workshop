PART 1: THE METHOD
============================================================

Every portrait prompt is built from 5 pieces, in this order:

  [QUEEN] + [BEAUTY DETAILS] + [POWER WORD] + [TYPOGRAPHY] + [VIBE]

Put together, it reads like this:

  "Ultra-detailed [VIBE] portrait of [QUEEN], a woman [AGE]
   with [SKIN] skin and a [BODY] figure, [HAIR], wearing
   [OUTFIT], [SETTING]. The word "[POWER WORD]" appears as
   [TYPOGRAPHY], [PLACEMENT]. Empowering, dignified and
   celebratory mood — strength and beauty, never pity.
   Radiant lighting, ultra detailed, 8k, professional
   photography, masterpiece."

That's the whole secret. Each bracket is a menu of choices.
Swap the choices, get a brand-new portrait. THAT is what
makes it a generator instead of a single prompt.

THE GOLDEN RULE (never break it):
Every description leads with BEAUTY, carries STRENGTH,
and never touches PITY. No sad words. No weepy words.
We celebrate — we don't mourn.

PART 2: THE 5 LESSONS
============================================================

LESSON 1: MEET THE UNSEEN (~10 min)
Objective: Fall in love with who we're celebrating and why.
- The 12 starter queens: DV Survivor, Alopecia Queen,
  Wheelchair Queen, Prosthetic Goddess, Vitiligo Beauty,
  Burn Survivor, Mastectomy Warrior, Deaf Queen, Blind Queen,
  Cane Queen, Plus-Size Goddess, Service Dog Duo.
- The reframe: these women are not "inspiring despite"
  anything — they are stunning, period.
- Activity: Pick the queen that speaks to YOU. Say why
  out loud or in the comments.

LESSON 2: ANATOMY OF A POWER PROMPT (~15 min)
Objective: Understand the 5-piece formula.
- Walk through one finished prompt and point to each piece:
  queen, beauty details, power word, typography, vibe.
- Show how swapping ONE piece (same queen, new typography)
  makes a totally different portrait.
- Key line: "The formula never changes. Only the choices do.
  That's what makes it a generator."
- Activity: Take the DV Survivor prompt and swap the power
  word SURVIVOR for UNBREAKABLE. Read the new prompt aloud.

LESSON 3: BUILD IT LIVE (~20 min)
Objective: Watch the generator get assembled from scratch.
- Start with the 12 queens as the foundation list.
- Add the beauty menus: skin, age, body, hair, outfit, setting.
- Add the 24 power words. Add the 21 typography styles.
- Add the 6 art vibes. Lock in the template.
- Generate the first live portraits together.
- Key line: "You're watching a product get born."

LESSON 4: MAKE IT YOURS (~15 min)
Objective: Customize — this is where it becomes THEIRS.
- The 3-part formula for a new queen (see Part 3).
- Live demo: take a suggestion from the audience and build
  a brand-new queen category on the spot.
- Remind them: start with my 12, but the list is theirs to grow.
- Activity: Everyone writes ONE new queen using the worksheet.

LESSON 5: GENERATE & GLOW (~15 min)
Objective: Create, share, celebrate.
- Everyone generates at least one portrait with their
  new queen (or a starter queen).
- Share in the comments/chat — the gallery moment.
- Close: "The unseen just got SEEN — by YOU."
- Send them home with the master prompt file.

PART 3: CREATE YOUR OWN QUEEN — THE WORKSHEET
============================================================

Copy this worksheet for every new category you add.

1. NAME HER
   Give the group a proud, celebratory name.
   Examples: Stroke Survivor ... Little Person Queen ...
   Chronic Illness Queen ... Autism Mom Warrior ...
   My queen: ___________________________________________

2. DESCRIBE HER WITH DIGNITY (1–2 sentences)
   Rules: beauty FIRST, strength ALWAYS, pity NEVER.
   Template: "a [uplifting adjective] [person],
   [what makes her radiant], [her strength]"
   GOOD: "a radiant queen living with chronic illness,
   her quiet strength glowing through every portrait,
   unbothered and luminous"
   BAD: "a poor sick woman suffering" (never like this)
   My description: ____________________________________
   ___________________________________________________

3. TEST HER
   Plug her into the template with a power word and a
   typography style. Read it aloud. Does she sound like
   a QUEEN? If yes — she's in. If anything sounds sad,
   rewrite until it shines.

4. ADD HER TO YOUR LIST
   Give her the next number on your queen list and use
   her exactly like the starters from now on.
