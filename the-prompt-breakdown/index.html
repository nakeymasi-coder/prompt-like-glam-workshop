FROM EPISODE 1

Your homework 📚

1

Pick your regular

Take one thing you ask your AI all the time — a caption, an email, a plan, anything.

2

Name the chair

Before you ask, start with “Act as a…” and name who it should be — a coach, a tutor, a caption writer.

3

Talk back

First answer not it? Adjust the chair. New role, sharper details. You are allowed to talk back to it.

What is The Prompt Breakdown?

A podcast that teaches you how to write prompts — the instructions you give an AI — in plain, simple language. Every episode breaks one prompt skill down so you can use it the same day.

Who is it for?

Beginners 40 and over who already use AI but feel like their answers come back weak, dry, or just… off. If you type a question and get back the cleaning-supplies answer, this show is yours.

What do I need to follow along?

Just whatever AI you already chat with and something you ask it all the time. Simone teaches, Desi tests it out loud — you try it right along with her.
